Support

Questions, a problem or a suggestion? Write to dishadorart@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. You can also use the feedback button inside the app, which opens a message to the same address.

Common questions

The times differ by a minute or two from my mosque.

Times are calculated on your device, so they can differ slightly from a local timetable. Use the minute adjustments for each prayer to match your mosque. You can also change the calculation method and choose standard or Hanafi Asr. The method is chosen automatically for your country, and you can change it.

I do not want to share my location.

You do not have to. Tap the city name on the Prayer Times tab and search for your city, and the app works without your location. When you do allow location, it is used only while you use the app, it stays on your device, and the developer never receives it.

I am not getting prayer notifications.

Check that notifications are allowed for the app in iPhone Settings, under Notifications. Prayer and reminder notifications are created on your device, so they do not need an internet connection. You can choose the adhan sound in the app.

How do I show the next prayer on my Lock Screen?

Switch on "Next prayer on the lock screen" in the Adjustments tab, under More. You can also add the app's Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and the Apple Watch app and complications, from the usual places on your devices.

Can I back up my data or move it to a new phone?

Yes. The Backup tab creates a file with your dhikr counts, zakat details, favorites, checklists and similar information. You decide where the file goes, for example Files or AirDrop. The developer never receives it.

Which languages are supported?

English and Albanian. There is a language switch inside the app.

How do I delete my data?

Your data stays on your device. Deleting the app removes the data it keeps on that device. Downloaded recordings can be deleted in the Fortress of the Muslim, and any backup file you created can be deleted by you.

The gold or currency prices do not load.

The Zakat calculator fetches prices from public services, so it needs an internet connection. If they do not load, you can type the prices yourself and the calculator works the same way.
