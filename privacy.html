Privacy Policy

Prayer Times: Islam (the app, shown as "Prayer Times" on your Home Screen)
Last updated: 2 November 2026
Developer: Dorart Disha
Contact: dishadorart@gmail.com

In short

The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It does not track you. The developer does not receive your location or any other personal information. Everything the app knows about you stays on your own devices.

What the app uses and why

Your location. The app asks for your location while you use it. It uses it to calculate prayer times, to show the Qibla direction and to show the name of your city. The location is stored on your device, and is shared only with the app's own widgets and its Apple Watch app on the same device. It is never sent to the developer. You can instead choose a city by hand in the app, and then the app does not need your location.

Place names. To show the name of your city, and to choose a suitable calculation method for your country, the app asks Apple's mapping services to look up the place for your coordinates. Searching for a city by name uses Apple's search in the same way. This is done by Apple, and Apple's privacy policy applies to it.

Notifications. Prayer, reminder and Ramadan notifications are created on your device. They are not sent from any server.

Gold, silver and currency prices. In the Zakat section, the app can fetch current gold and silver prices and exchange rates from the public services api.gold-api.com and open.er-api.com. The requests contain only the metal or currency being asked for. Like any website, those services can see your internet address. You can type prices yourself instead.

Mosques nearby. When you open Mosques Nearby, the app searches for mosques around your location. It asks Apple Maps, and it asks the public OpenStreetMap services overpass-api.de and overpass.kumi.systems (the app asks both at once and uses the first good answer). The OpenStreetMap requests contain your coordinates and the search distance, and nothing else. Like any website, those services can see your internet address. Your favorite mosques are stored on your device only.

Audio recordings. If you play or download a recording in the Fortress of the Muslim, the app downloads it from mburojaemuslimanit.com or hisnmuslim.com. Those websites can see your internet address, as any website can. Nothing about you is sent along with the request. Downloaded recordings are kept on your device and can be deleted in the app.

Apple Watch and widgets. The app shares your location, settings and prayer times with its own widgets and its Apple Watch app, using the services Apple provides for that on your devices.

Your worship data. Dhikr counts, your zakat details and payments, favorites, checklists, the Quran plan and similar information are stored only on your device. The Backup tab can create a file with this data. The file is created by you, and you decide where it goes (for example Files or AirDrop). The developer never receives it.

Feedback. If you use the feedback button, the app opens your own Mail app with a message to the developer's address. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create an account, and it does not ask for your name or email address.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and it contains no third-party software that collects data.
  • It does not sell or share personal information.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting the app removes the data the app keeps on that device. Recordings can be deleted in the Fortress of the Muslim, and the backup file you created can be deleted by you.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
